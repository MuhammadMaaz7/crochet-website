# Catalogue direction — 29 October 2026

## What the evidence supports
Etsy's Spring/Summer 2026 report describes interest in handmade texture and personalised occasions, but its comparison data is from US Etsy activity as of February 2026. This is directional context, not proof of Pakistani demand for PhotoCraftSquare.

Pakistan retailer Zoha Collection lists several larger crochet bags around PKR 2,800–4,000, with small charms around PKR 300–450. These are competitors' asking prices, not verified sales, profit or recommended PhotoCraftSquare prices. Local gajray listings show a defined wedding/Mehndi use case. PhotoCraftSquare's own archive contains photographed bags, gajray and made-to-order birthday bunting, making these stronger near-term catalogue candidates than invented products.

## Merchandising decision
Lead with the mocha bow tote, gajray, coordinated dining set and earth-tone shoulder bag. Show the cactus bag and personalisable bunting as distinctive choices. Keep keyrings as smaller gifts/add-ons rather than the lead collection. Add an Occasion category and celebration suggestions. Twenty designs are now public, including six additions in this pass: two gajra designs, three bags and bunting.

This is a commercial hypothesis. Track product views, qualified quote requests, accepted quotes, average order value, maker hours per order and completed sales. Avoid calling any design a bestseller without actual sales data.

## Offer design
Gajray: individual quantities, with single/pair/group shortcuts; confirm wrist measurement, fastening, event date, making capacity and colour palette. Photos showing pairs do not mean the default price/quantity includes two. Both server quantity snapshots and customer labels count individual gajray.

Bunting: ask for wording; agree flag count, flag size, cord length and colours before quoting. Main photo is an actual lettered-flag detail, not a promise of an invented full banner.

Bags: agree dimensions, lining, closure, strap drop and load before acceptance. Open crochet is not claimed to secure loose valuables or protect a laptop. Totes may have higher selling prices but also take longer to make.

## Colour and image treatment
Product-specific prepared colour concepts are visible on collection cards and product pages; selecting one on a card carries its palette into the product page. They are approximate yarn previews and need material confirmation. Actual design changes or arbitrary custom colours do not produce a fake live product photo. Replacement base photos disable stale packs.

The tissue covers use consistent square framing and similar visual scale. Existing observed perspectives are preserved; no unseen reverse/side geometry is invented to force identical angles. A real matched-angle shoot remains the best final catalogue photography method.

## Price and capacity before promotion
Set a labour-inclusive minimum: materials + maker time at a chosen hourly rate + packaging + transaction/delivery costs borne by the shop + overhead allowance + profit. Check contribution per maker hour, not just selling price. Do not import a competitor's price or historical Instagram price as a current offer. Agree current prices or useful starting ranges and making times before paid acquisition.
